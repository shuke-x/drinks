## 中文

### 内容规则

公开配方、名称、故事、图片和品饮分享须与服务用途相关。不得提交违法内容、对他人的威胁或骚扰、仇恨或露骨色情内容、侵犯知识产权或隐私的内容、垃圾信息、恶意链接、虚假身份或鼓励危险饮酒的内容。不得发布未获同意的他人照片、联系方式或其他个人资料。私人内容也须遵守法律及系统安全要求。

### 如何举报或申诉

将邮件发至 **shybro027@gmail.com**，主题写“Tonight Drinks 内容举报”或“Tonight Drinks 申诉”。请提供内容名称、链接或 ID、问题类别、简要说明及必要证据；不必发送身份证件、密码或无关的敏感资料。如涉及紧急人身危险，请同时联系所在地紧急服务。

运营方应实际监看该邮箱，记录举报，按严重程度及时核查，可隐藏或删除内容、限制账户并通知相关人员。对明显违法或迫切危险的内容应优先处置。被处置者可通过同一邮箱申诉；我们会按具体情况复核。为保护举报人和他人隐私，我们可能不能公开全部处理细节。

**上架前功能条件**：应在应用内提供针对单条公开内容的举报入口，以及用户屏蔽滥用用户的能力，并验证审核过滤、通知和受理流程正常运作。当前客户端未确认具备这些入口；本文件不能替代产品功能。

## English

### Content rules

Public recipes, names, stories, images, and tasting shares must be relevant to the service. Do not submit unlawful material, threats or harassment, hateful or sexually explicit material, content that infringes intellectual property or privacy, spam, malicious links, impersonation, or content encouraging dangerous drinking. Do not post another person's photo, contact details, or other personal information without permission. Private content must also comply with law and service security requirements.

### Reports and appeals

Email **shybro027@gmail.com** with “Tonight Drinks content report” or “Tonight Drinks appeal” in the subject. Include the content name, link or ID, the issue category, a short explanation, and relevant evidence. Do not send identity documents, passwords, or unrelated sensitive data. If there is an immediate risk to someone's safety, also contact local emergency services.

The operator must actually monitor this mailbox, log reports, review them promptly according to severity, and where appropriate hide or remove content, restrict accounts, and notify affected people. Clearly unlawful or urgent dangerous content should be prioritized. An affected user may appeal to the same address. We may be unable to disclose every detail of an outcome in order to protect privacy.

**Release condition:** provide in-app reporting for individual public items and a way for users to block abusive users, and verify that filtering, moderation notices, and response operations work. The current client has not been shown to provide these controls; this document cannot replace product functionality.
